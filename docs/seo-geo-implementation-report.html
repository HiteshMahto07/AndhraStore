The site has a solid technical foundation: per-page canonical tags, OG/Twitter cards, Product schema on all 14 product pages, Organization schema globally, a sitemap covering 21 URLs, and security headers in place. The critical gap is content depth — product pages average ~70 words of body copy, zero blog content exists, and FAQ schema is absent everywhere. The keyword research identifies ~7,300 searches/month of informational volume that currently has no landing page to capture it.

FAQ Questions to Add (GEO + Informational Targets)

Are Andhra pickles vegetarian?
Andhra Store offers both vegetarian and non-vegetarian pickles. Our 10 vegetarian varieties include Mango Avakaya, Gongura, Garlic, Ginger, Lemon, Tomato, Red Chilli, Green Chilli, Curry Leaves, and Amla. Our 4 non-vegetarian varieties are Chicken, Meat, Prawns, and Fish.
How long do Andhra pickles last?
Our Andhra pickles last 6 to 12 months when stored in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. Refrigerate after opening and use a dry spoon. Because we use no preservatives, always check storage instructions on the jar.
Do you deliver Andhra pickles across India?
Yes, we ship all 14 pickles pan-India, including to Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, and Delhi. Orders are dispatched within 2 business days.
What oil is used in Andhra pickles?
We use cold-pressed sesame oil and cold-pressed mustard oil in our Andhra pickles. Cold pressing preserves the natural flavour and nutrients of the oil without chemical refining.
